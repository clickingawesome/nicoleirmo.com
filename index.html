Story sells.

Every brand already has a good story. Most just bury it under jargon, fine print and stock photos.

Uncover the brand. Build the team. Hit the gas.

Great marketing is truth x hope.

The truth about what a product really does, and the hope of what life looks like with it, bundled into a story people can connect to.

My passion is finding that story and figuring out where it should be told and who should tell it. Sometimes that's a TV spot. Sometimes it's a customer brave enough to share their own. Then I build the team and systems that keep telling it.

Home / About

About.

15+ years of building marketing from scratch, and I still love what I do.

By day, I'm VP of Marketing at NorthShore Care Supply, a DTC medical supply and CPG brand. I lead marketing and ecommerce, report to the founder and CEO, and run a nine-person team, most of whom I hired. By night, I'm usually still thinking about marketing. My husband is a marketer too, so dinner is basically a brainstorm session, and our teenager is already plotting his first subscription business.

My story.

I'm an intentional optimist, and I love that marketing gets to shape how people see things, including themselves. Done right, it can inspire, empower and give people hope. That's kind of magical.

NorthShore started as a client of my consultancy, Clicking Awesome. In 2023 they recruited me in-house as Director of Marketing. Within about ninety days I was running marketing for the whole business, and in 2024 I was promoted to VP. I started Clicking Awesome by choice after Focus Products was acquired and spent two years working across five industries. Before that, I led marketing for hospitality and healthcare product brands, earning a promotion at every stop.

I've marketed a lot of different things, but product marketing is my favorite, DTC or B2B. There's nothing like selling something people can actually hold in their hands.

Every role follows the same playbook. Find the story the brand isn't telling. Tell it clearly, everywhere. Build the team and systems that keep it going. Lately that playbook includes AI: I'm leading adoption across my team, from choosing the platforms to setting the standards to building the workflows. Here's a secret: I built this whole website with AI. Sure beats the old WordPress days.

I'm a leader who still likes getting my hands dirty. I set the strategy, and I'm just as happy rolling up my sleeves to help get it done. I can see the forest and the trees.

I never expected to be this excited about adult diapers, but here we are. I love a complicated category (I've done bed bugs, too).

One of those rare leaders who could do every creative function herself, yet was smart enough to know when to lead, delegate, and develop the people around her.

Amy Vestal, RN · Founder & CRO · Cross-functional colleague
