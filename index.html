About.

Twelve years of building marketing functions that didn't exist yet.

I lead marketing and ecommerce for a direct-to-consumer medical supply brand, reporting to the founder and CEO, with a nine-person marketing and ecommerce team, most of whom I hired.

Marketing is a big part of how we interact with the world, which is why I care about building brands and content people actually engage with. Done well, it turns a good product into something that pops, inspires and sells. What I love most is the puzzle: bringing many pieces together to show the big picture. That is how I work with a brand, leading cross-functional collaboration across marketing, sales, ecommerce and the executive team to connect the pieces and strengthen what the brand is worth.

NorthShore was a consulting client first. When they recruited me in-house in 2023, the practice concluded and I took on marketing for the whole business within about ninety days. Before that I ran an independent consultancy across five industries, by choice, after Focus Products was acquired, and before that I led marketing for hospitality and healthcare product brands, with a promotion at every stop.

The pattern in every role is the same: find the story the brand is failing to tell, tell it clearly everywhere, then build the team and the systems that keep telling it. Right now that includes leading adoption of AI tools across the marketing team: selecting platforms, setting usage standards and building the workflows.

I never expected to be this excited about adult diapers, but here we are. I like complicated categories (I have done bed bugs too), I have been recognized for all four of our company values, I captain the MS Walk team, and my aim in every role is to leave marketing better than I found it.
