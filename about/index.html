About Nicole Irmo.

Marketing executive who turns brand positioning into commercial results, and builds the teams that sustain it.

Nicole Irmo is a branding nerd who leads marketing and ecommerce for a direct-to-consumer medical supply brand, where she repositioned a 20-year-old marketplace into a brand platform and built the team that sustains it. She previously ran an independent consultancy and led marketing for hospitality and healthcare product brands. She likes complicated categories, and her aim in every role is to leave marketing better than she found it.

Career

  • 2023 to present: Marketing leader, DTC medical supply and CPG brand (Director of Marketing, then Vice President, Marketing).
  • 2021 to 2023: Independent marketing consultant.
  • 2015 to 2021: Marketing Director, hospitality and healthcare products manufacturer.
  • 2014 to 2015: Marketing Manager, foodservice equipment manufacturer.
